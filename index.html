⚙️ How It Works
1

Environment Simulates a Farm

Server models soil moisture via FAO-56 water balance, crop growth via GDD, weather from 3 Indian regions, and pest populations using ICAR thresholds — updated every simulated day.

2

LLM Agent Makes Daily Decisions

An AI agent reads moisture levels, pest counts, 7-day forecasts, and market prices — then decides whether to irrigate, spray pesticides, or harvest.

3

Dense Rewards from Real Science

Every decision scores against FAO-56 equations, ICAR economic thresholds, and AGMARKNET prices. Dense per-step rewards in [−1.0, 1.0] — zero arbitrary heuristics.

📚 Scientific References & Grounding
💧

Allen et al. (1998) — FAO-56 Penman-Monteith

Daily crop water demand calculation via ET₀ × Kc. Used for all irrigation scheduling reward functions and soil water balance equations across all tasks.

🦗

ICAR IPM Guidelines (2022)

Economic Thresholds (ET) and Economic Injury Levels (EIL) for whitefly, bollworm, brown planthopper, stem borer, and fruit borer in Indian conditions.

📊

AGMARKNET Historical Price Data 2023–24

Wholesale market price data used for monthly price indices, seasonal volatility profiles, grade premiums, and harvest glut discount modelling.

🏛️

CACP MSP Recommendations 2023–24

Commission for Agricultural Costs & Prices official Minimum Support Prices used as price floor benchmarks in season optimiser reward calculations.

🌿

Baret & Guyot (1991) — NDVI-LAI Relationship

Beer-Lambert law applied to NDVI-LAI relationship for crop canopy monitoring, growth stage estimation, and yield potential tracking throughout the season.

🌦️

IMD 30-Year Regional Climate Normals

India Meteorological Department normals for Pune (MH), Ludhiana (PB), and Guntur (AP) used as stochastic weather simulation seeds with realistic uncertainty.

⚡ Reward Structure

[−1.0, +1.0]

Dense per-step rewards provide continuous feedback. Positive for correct irrigation, timely pest intervention, optimal harvest timing. Negative for water stress, unnecessary sprays, pesticide resistance, waterlogging, and premature or late harvest.
